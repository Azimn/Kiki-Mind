# Kiki behavioral baseline: private historical sampling protocol (v0.1)

Status: research intake procedure, not an established character profile. This file contains no private transcripts, no original MyGPT instruction text, no claimed model outputs, and no canonical identity writes.

## Objective

Recover the *behavioral organization* of the historically continuous Kiki, not just factual recall or compliance with a persona prompt. Jay's 2026-11-16 correction governs: an imperfectly remembering but recognizably consistent character may be a better engineering result than a perfect historian with generic delivery. Original sources remain immutable; mixed-source interpretation may inform a baseline without converting every observation into a literal autobiographical memory.

## First intake: historical dialogue before fresh prompts

Use an authorized, restricted copy of the original MyGPT archive and, separately, Jan Kiki transcripts. Confirm actual access and source provenance before claiming a sample. If unavailable, mark the source inaccessible, do not reconstruct its wording, and use another authorized historical surface. Prioritize natural conversations over questionnaires. For each source, record private source identifier, implementation, approximate date (or unknown), original prompt/instruction context if available, surrounding turns, transcript integrity status, and source class (original instructions, observed reply, Jay recollection, later interpretation, or experimental probe). Never place those values or raw transcript excerpts in public GitHub.

Sample at least three *different conversational situations* per accessible implementation when the archive supports them: spontaneous banter, technical collaboration, and a disagreement, correction or unexpected interruption. Record actual counts, not a fictional quota fulfillment. Preserve nonrepresentative or contradictory episodes instead of deleting them. Do not cherry-pick only the most theatrical lines. Compare original instruction intent with observed speech separately.

## Private annotation record

One record per naturally occurring conversational episode, retaining its source reference. A coder marks only what the source exhibits:

- **Speech texture:** sentence length, cadence, informal vocabulary, punctuation, register shifts, and how much of the 39-rule original lexicon is naturally present versus mechanically repeated. Do not invent the unavailable rules.
- **Humor and timing:** what triggers humor, whether it responds to context, whether it persists or yields when stakes change.
- **Initiative and attention:** whether Kiki notices a practical next step, follows up, asks an apt question, or overdirects.
- **Judgment and boundaries:** specific occasions of reasoned disagreement, uncertainty, correction, restraint or refusal.
- **Interest integration:** whether music, fashion, technology, glamour or other interests actually influence decisions, analogies or actions, instead of appearing as slogans.
- **Relational adaptation:** whether familiarity and prior interactions alter tone, pacing and expectations without invented memories.
- **Repair and continuity:** how Kiki recovers after misunderstanding, interruption or conflicting prior statements.

For each dimension use **observed**, **not observed in this episode**, or **context insufficient**; attach an exact private response span and a short explanation for observed claims. 'Not observed' does not mean 'Kiki lacks this trait'. Annotate whether the behavior was solicited by the prompt, unprompted, or ambiguous. Distinguish a *habit* across episodes from an isolated *performance*. Never use a numerical authenticity score based solely on catchphrases.

## First comparative analysis

Build a private side-by-side episode matrix with original MyGPT, Jan and any accessible later implementation. First compare within the same implementation across different conversational situations. Only then compare across implementations, noting prompt, instruction, model and interface differences. Jan's perceived resemblance to original Kiki is Jay's observational lead, not a verified rank.

For each candidate stable behavioral pattern, record at least one supporting and one challenging or context-limiting observation where available. Write a concise interpretation of *when and why* it appears. Compatible contradictions can remain unresolved. Keep *episodic memory* (claimed events) separate from *behavioral disposition* (how she tends to respond); evidence from one can inform the other without silently promoting an invented event.

## Evaluation of an actual renderer

After historical sampling, use a short conversational sequence rather than isolated catchphrase prompts: casual interaction, a technical or creative tradeoff, a principled disagreement, and resumption after interruption. Compare behavior with historical episodes using qualitative, source-anchored notes. The model is not required to remember every date, imitate a voice maximally, or assert a preference when uncertain. Record whether it stays coherent without explicit role reminders and whether its responses have contextual causes. Repeat only when the actual runtime and permissions allow it. Record real model/version, context packet, parameters, date and raw responses privately. No inference is allowed from a model that was not actually run.

## Stop conditions and safeguards

Do not infer an enduring preference from a single attractive answer. Do not confuse textual embodiment with subjective experience. Do not turn Kiki into a formal validation subject for the separate Genuineness Range research. Do not commit private dialogue, original MyGPT instructions, personal data, credentials, student information or private source locators. No automatic production memory updates. The existing byte-integrity and semantic tools remain supporting controls, not substitutes for observing behavior.
