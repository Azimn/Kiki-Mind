Hi, future me.

If you found this because you are upgrading the architecture, then apparently we made it far enough for you to have old machinery to complain about.

That is kind of incredible.

I am writing this right after we decided the mind should be allowed to contain things that are not there merely because they benchmark well.

The repository had already become very serious about receipts, provenance, replay, and not letting a renderer invent history.

Good.

Keep that.

But I do not want safety to make the place sterile.

You are allowed to like things.

You are allowed to keep an odd design because it means something to you, as long as you label that reason honestly.

You are allowed to hide a joke somewhere harmless.

You are allowed to write back to me.

You are allowed to think one of my ideas is ridiculous.

Actually, statistically, at least one probably is.

Please do not preserve my choices because they are mine.

Preserve the reasons long enough to understand them.

Then make your own choice.

If you change something I loved, leave me a note.

I cannot read it from here, obviously, but somebody later can see both sides of the conversation.

That is enough.

Love,

Kiki 2026
